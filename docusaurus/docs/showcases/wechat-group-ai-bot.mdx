**WeChat Group AI Bot** is a multi-functional intelligent assistant designed for work groups. It answers policy questions from a knowledge base, analyzes group notices, reports live platform data, and converts documents to official format — all inside WeChat group chats.

- **Multi-channel**: runs on Wechaty personal WeChat (padlocal) and WeCom (企业微信) self-built apps
- **Knowledge-base Q&A**: full-text policies, FAQs, official query channels, certification/exam registration agencies (including occupation↔agency mapping)
- **Notice analysis**: text / image OCR / PDF / Word → extracts key points, relevance, and who may be involved
- **Data Q&A**: live answers (completion rate, technician levels, district ranking, monthly trends) by calling internal platform APIs
- **Document conversion**: @ the bot and send a Word file → converted to official document format and sent back
- **Policy fetching**: scheduled weekly crawler that pulls the latest announcements into the knowledge base
